About Us

It started with one sewing machine and my uncle's tailoring shop.

In 2012, my uncle let me use a corner of his tailoring shop and one old machine. I made 20 school bags for a neighbour's children. Two of those straps tore in a month, and I remade every one of them for free. That day I decided that no Baxora bag would ever fail at the seam.

Today we run our own small unit with trained cutters and stitchers. We buy fabric, zips and foam in bulk, test every strap by hand, and finish each bag ourselves. We do not resell other people's bags.

Whether you want one bag for your child or five hundred for your school, you will talk to me or my team directly, with no agents in between.

  • We are the manufacturer, so you get factory price
  • Double-stitched stress points on straps and handles
  • Branded zips and thick lining as standard
  • Free repair of stitching defects for 6 months
  • Your logo, your colours, your quantity
